Power Bus Interface

The Spectrometer receives unregulated 28V power from the spacecraft bus through connector J1. The power interface provides over-current protection via a 5A fuse on the spacecraft side. The Spectrometer internal power conditioning unit (CPC) regulates 28V to the required secondary voltages (5V, ±12V, -190V detector bias).

Power Modes

Science Mode: 88.5W average (cryocooler dominant)

Eclipse Mode: 17.5W (survival heaters + IDPU standby)

Safehold Mode: 7.0W (essential heaters only)

The spacecraft must provide continuous power in all modes. Power cycling the Spectrometer requires 48+ hours for cryocooler re-cooldown.

Current Requirements

Inrush current at power-on: < 10A for < 100ms

Steady-state science mode: 3.2A at 28V

Peak transient (shutter actuation): 5A for < 50ms

The spacecraft bus must accommodate these transient loads without voltage droop below 24V.
